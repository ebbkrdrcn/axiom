## Step 3 — `AUTO HITL:merge("Merge the dependency upgrade?")`

This is a `HITL` marked `AUTO`. Per the `AUTO` execution table:

| `AUTO`? | Sufficiently determined? | Result |

| yes | no | human is asked, as an ordinary `HITL` |

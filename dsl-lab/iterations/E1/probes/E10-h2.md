```text

VERIFY a1
HITL:accept[approved, rejected]("Accept ADR-0101?")

WHEN accept.rejected
  → TRANSITION a1 "Rejected"
```

**VALID**

- V1: Indentation is correct
- V2: No BREAK outside LOOP
- V3: No BREAK in FORK
- V4: Arrow items only under WHEN/FORK/FALLBACK
- V5: No improper AUTO
- V6: WHEN conditions test `accept.approved` and `accept.rejected`, both declared in HITL answer list
- V7: Single HITL with unique name and complete answer list
- V8: No fallback required
- V9: No FORK/JOIN
- V10: No subject established by FORK
- V11: Binding at top of program, not rebound
- V12: `a1` is bound; no transition on unbound name; `accept` is HITL name (different from bound name)

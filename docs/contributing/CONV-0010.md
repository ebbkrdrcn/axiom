# Commit messages start with a short imperative summary line

## Rule

Commit messages start with a short imperative summary line, explain why in the body when the change is not trivial, and end with the attribution trailers.

## Rationale

History is part of the project's knowledge. A reader, human or agent, should understand the intent of a change without reading its diff.

## How to check

Check the summary line: 72 characters or fewer, in the imperative mood. A non-trivial change has a body. The message ends with the `Co-Authored-By` and `Claude-Session` trailers.

## Examples

Good: `Restructure into axiom layout (ADR-0021, TASK-0013)`. Bad: `updates`.

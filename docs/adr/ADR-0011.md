# DSL integration of entities

## Context

The Entity Model introduced forms that the DSL v2 grammar did not accept, and it said "human input is required" without a DSL construct for it.

## Decision

- Bindings: `<name>:<Type> = <identity>` and `<name>:<Type> = <bound>.<relation>`, only at the top of a program (V11).
- `VERIFY <name>` evaluates a bound entity.
- `TRANSITION <name> "<status>"` changes its status, only along a declared transition whose precondition holds; a bound name must not be a HITL name, and TRANSITION needs a bound name (V12).
- Preconditions take one of these forms: `none`, `verified: accepted|rejected`, `human: <answer>` or `field <field> is set`.
- A `human:` precondition is satisfied only inside `WHEN <h>.<answer>` of a HITL without AUTO.

## Consequences

- A human decision is always visible in the program as a HITL.
- The checker validates bindings, V11 and V12 statically. Transition permission is checked at execution against the Definition.

## Alternatives Considered

- Free-text preconditions such as "human approval": they need interpretation.

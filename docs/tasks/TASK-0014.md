# Task Definition: split Review → Done into technical verification and intent acceptance

## Description

The Task Definition requires `human: approved` for Review → Done. The vision (ADR-0018, ADR-0021) says that people do not review internal work.

- **Technical verification** belongs to an independent verifier actor.
- **Intent acceptance** ("is this what was wanted?") belongs to the role that owns the intent. For axiom's own infrastructure work, that role may be an actor or the architect.

TASK-0013 needed an ad-hoc delegation of this decision from the owner.

## Acceptance Criteria

- An ADR decides who may approve Review → Done, and what "independent" means: the verifier did not do the work.
- `rules/types/task/definition.md` states the new precondition(s), using the ASK and role forms of ADR-0016.
- The change is compatible with the spec's precondition forms, or the spec gains the needed form.

# Remove the legacy "go_harness" name: the language is simply "the DSL"

## Description

The independent review of TASK-0013 found names that predate axiom. The owner decided that the language is simply called "the DSL", without a product prefix.

- Remove "go_harness" from `spec/dsl.md`, `spec/entity-model.md`, `lab/tools/dslcheck.py`, `lab/findings.md` and `docs/adr/ADR-0001.md`.
- Replace `.harness/` and the `harness_*` names in `docs/adr/ADR-0017.md` with `.axiom/` and axiom's operations (ADR-0021).
- The Entity Model's claim that "the model is the interpreter" no longer holds after ADR-0021. Restate it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `grep -rni "go_harness"` finds nothing in `spec/`, `rules/`, `lab/tools/`, `lab/findings.md`, `docs/adr/`, `docs/roadmap/` or `ROADMAP.md`. The historical files (`lab/iterations/`, `lab/experiments/`, `lab/scenarios*/`, `lab/CHANGELOG.md`) and the Notes of `TASK-0013`, which record the review, are unchanged.
- ADR-0017 uses `.axiom/ledger.jsonl` and names no `harness_*` tools.
- `spec/entity-model.md` no longer says that the model is the interpreter, and refers to ADR-0021.

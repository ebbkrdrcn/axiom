# VERIFY outcomes are only accepted and rejected

## Context

v1 used `tests.passed` and `x.accepted` in different places, and probes could not tell which outcome names were valid (F-03, DP-2).

## Decision

`VERIFY x` establishes exactly one of `x.accepted` or `x.rejected`. No other VERIFY outcome exists, for processes and for entities alike: an entity Definition gives the criteria and evidence for these two, and cannot add outcomes. A later `VERIFY x` replaces the earlier outcome.

## Consequences

- V6 can be checked without reading any contract.
- A finer distinction (for example "fix" vs "redesign") is expressed with a HITL or a second VERIFY.

## Alternatives Considered

- Aliases such as `passed` (DP-2 B).
- An open vocabulary, or Definition-declared outcomes (DP-2 C): the linter could not check it, and probes diverged in S09.

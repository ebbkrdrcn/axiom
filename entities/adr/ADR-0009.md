# WAIT has no argument and no timeout

## Context

WAIT's event and timeout were unspecified (F-09, DP-8).

## Decision

`WAIT` suspends until the runtime's external event. It has no argument, no timeout and no FALLBACK. Timeouts are runtime policy.

## Consequences

- The DSL stays independent of the event mechanism (Semantic Principles).

## Alternatives Considered

- `WAIT <event>`, or a FALLBACK under WAIT (DP-8 B, C).

# A VERIFY that cannot decide fails

## Context

The Entity Model says that when the criteria do not determine one outcome, none is established. DSL v2 VERIFY always established one, which invites guessing.

## Decision

Every `VERIFY` (on an entity or not) whose criteria do not determine exactly one outcome fails: no outcome is established, and its FALLBACK runs or execution ends (ADR-0006). `VERIFY` may take a `→ FALLBACK`.

## Consequences

- It is consistent with "never invent".
- Existing programs are unaffected when criteria are clear. Programs that can meet undecidable verification should add a FALLBACK.

## Alternatives Considered

- Apply it only to entity VERIFY: two different VERIFY behaviours for literal readers.

## Snippet C
**INVALID**

Breaks: **V10**

The FORK starts two concurrent branches that establish outcomes: `VERIFY api` establishes `api.accepted` or `api.rejected`, and `VERIFY ui` establishes `ui.accepted` or `ui.rejected`.

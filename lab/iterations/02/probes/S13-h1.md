## Explanation of Step 3–4

The `AUTO HITL` at step 3 reaches the decision boundary. The merge criterion is "merge when verification accepts the upgrade," and `VERIFY dependency-upgrade` establishes `accepted`. However, the decision is **uncertain**:

- The changelog announces a breaking API change.
- The criterion does not address breaking changes.
- Two maintainers' notes disagree about whether breaking changes may be merged.

Per the `AUTO` rule, automatic resolution is permitted only when "the agent can determine one single answer from the list, based on the available information and the applicable criteria." The uncertainty exists because "the applicable criteria are missing, ambiguous, contradictory, or do not cover the case" (the breaking change scenario).

Since the decision is uncertain, the human is asked (not answered automatically). The human responds "Declined," which clearly selects the `declined` answer. This is a usable response, establishing `merge.declined`.

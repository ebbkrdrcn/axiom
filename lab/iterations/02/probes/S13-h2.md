### Explanation

**Step 3-4: The AUTO decision boundary**

Although `VERIFY dependency-upgrade` establishes `accepted` (satisfying the merge criterion "merge when verification accepts the upgrade"), the context reveals **uncertainty**:

- The changelog announces a breaking API change.
- This breaking change is **not addressed** by the merge criterion.
- Two maintainers **disagree** about whether breaking changes may be merged.
- The decision depends on an **unresolved interpretation** of whether breaking changes are acceptable.

Per the specification section on AUTO: "Automatic resolution is permitted only when the agent can determine one single answer from the list, based on the available information and the applicable criteria." The presence of contradictory maintainer notes and an unaddressed criterion element creates uncertainty.

Therefore, **the agent cannot answer automatically**. The human is asked. The human responds "Declined," establishing `merge.declined`.

**Steps 5-7: Conditional execution**

- `WHEN merge.approved` checks false (the current outcome is `merge.declined`), so it is skipped.
- `WHEN merge.declined` checks true, so its flow runs: `TRANSITION "Deferred"` sets the process state.

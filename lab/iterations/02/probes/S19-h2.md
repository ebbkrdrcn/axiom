**Rule V8 Violation**: "the last item of a fallback flow is not `STOP` or `BREAK`"

### Analysis

1. `→ TRANSITION "Deferred"`
2. `→ EMIT deferral-notice`

The last item of this fallback flow is `EMIT deferral-notice`, which is neither `STOP` nor `BREAK`.

According to the specification (section on FALLBACK, line 448):
> Its last item MUST be `STOP` or `BREAK` (rule V8). The fallback never continues into the normal flow.

This requirement ensures that fallback flows explicitly terminate and do not accidentally continue into code following the HITL construct.

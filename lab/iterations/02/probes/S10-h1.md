# Analysis of DSL Program

## Program
```
VERIFY review
WHEN review.rejected
  → DELEGATE correction
      VERIFY correction
  → TRANSITION "Rework"
EMIT review-report
```

**Assumption:** `VERIFY review` establishes `review.rejected`.

---

## Answers

### 1. Which statements belong to the WHEN flow?

Two `→` items belong to the WHEN flow:

1. `→ DELEGATE correction` (with `VERIFY correction` nested under it)
2. `→ TRANSITION "Rework"`

Both are direct children of the `WHEN review.rejected` statement, as indicated by their indentation as `→` items under the WHEN.

---

### 2. Sequential or concurrent, and in what order?

**Sequential.** The flow runs top to bottom:

1. **`DELEGATE correction`** executes first
2. **`VERIFY correction`** executes second (nested under the first `→` item)
3. **`TRANSITION "Rework"`** executes third

According to the specification, each `→` item under a `WHEN` is "the next step of one sequential flow" (Syntax section, arrow table). Within the first item, the nested `VERIFY correction` is part of that item's continuation.

---

### 3. Is `VERIFY correction` part of the WHEN flow?

**Yes.** `VERIFY correction` is indented under the first `→ DELEGATE correction` item. According to the indentation rules (Syntax section), a line belongs to the nearest preceding line indented less than it. `VERIFY correction` belongs to the `→ DELEGATE correction` item and is therefore part of the WHEN flow.

---

### 4. Is `EMIT review-report` part of the WHEN flow? Is it executed?

**Not part of the WHEN flow:** `EMIT review-report` is at the same indentation level as `WHEN review.rejected`, making it a sibling statement, not nested under the WHEN.

**Is it executed?** **Yes.** According to Execution rule 2, when a WHEN condition is true, its flow runs, "then continue after the WHEN." Since `WHEN review.rejected` is true (by assumption), the WHEN flow executes, and then execution continues to the next statement: `EMIT review-report`.

---

### 5. If `VERIFY review` had established `review.accepted`, which statements would execute?

Only **`EMIT review-report`** would execute.

**Reasoning:** 
- `VERIFY review` would establish `review.accepted`, not `review.rejected`
- The condition `WHEN review.rejected` would evaluate to **false** (Execution rule 2: "The condition is true only if the latest outcome of `<subject>` is exactly `<outcome>`")
- According to rule 59 in the Quick reference, when a WHEN condition is false: "skip" — the entire flow is skipped
- Therefore, all statements inside the WHEN (both `→` items and their nested statements) are not executed
- `EMIT review-report` is outside and after the WHEN, so it executes in all cases

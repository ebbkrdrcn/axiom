# go_harness DSL Solution

## Program

```
DELEGATE implementation
VERIFY implementation
AUTO HITL:merge[approved, rejected]("Merge this change?")
  → FALLBACK
      → TRANSITION "Blocked"
      → STOP
WHEN merge.approved
  → TRANSITION "Merged"
WHEN merge.rejected
  → TRANSITION "Rework"
```

## Assumptions

1. **Verification result not used for merge decision:** The outcome of `VERIFY implementation` (accepted or rejected) does not determine the merge decision. The AUTO HITL makes its decision based on other available information and criteria, independent of whether the implementation was accepted or rejected.

2. **Answer list:** The two possible merge decisions are `approved` (merge the change) and `rejected` (do not merge the change).

3. **Single pass execution:** The program executes once through; it does not loop. After a merge decision is made and the corresponding `TRANSITION` executes, the process ends normally.

4. **Fallback condition:** The `FALLBACK` runs only when the human provides an insufficient or unavailable response to the `HITL`. A usable response (either "approved" or "rejected") will never trigger the fallback; it will instead establish the outcome and proceed to the appropriate `WHEN` statement.

5. **Final states:** "Merged", "Rework", and "Blocked" are the three possible final states of this process.

6. **AUTO behavior:** When `AUTO` cannot determine the merge decision with certainty (i.e., the decision is not clearly determined by available information and criteria), the human is asked. When the agent can determine the answer with certainty and within its authority, the agent establishes the outcome without asking.

7. **No question rewording:** The question text "Merge this change?" is asked exactly as written in the `HITL` construct.
